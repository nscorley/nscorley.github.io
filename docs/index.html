Nate Corley

I’m a PhD student in David Baker’s lab at the University of Washington’s Institute for Protein Design.

I work on generative modeling for protein structure prediction and design. I’m interested in how we can model molecular structure and interactions to design proteins with new functions.

Previously, I worked at McKinsey & Company and Microsoft.

Research

Biomolecular design through latent reasoning with RFD4-Proteina

Nathaniel Corley, Kieran Didi, Maxwell W. Kazman, Rafael I. Brent, et al.
bioRxiv, 2026

I led development of RFD4-Proteina, a generative model for joint sequence and structure design in a chemical and conformational latent space. RFD4 delivered a step change in design quality and computational efficiency, setting a new Pareto frontier across protein binding, small-molecule binding, enzyme scaffolding, and DNA binding benchmarks.

We experimentally validated RFD4-Proteina across selective molecular binding and catalysis, including enzymes for both bond cleavage and bond formation and a proof of concept for pharmaceutical synthesis.

PaperProject

Accelerating Biomolecular Modeling with AtomWorks and RF3

Nathaniel Corley, Simon Mathis, Rohith Krishna, et al.
bioRxiv, 2025

I led development of AtomWorks and the structure prediction model RosettaFold3 (RF3), which achieved state-of-the-art open-source performance on protein–protein, protein–ligand, and mixed-chirality peptide structure prediction at release.

AtomWorks has been adopted by hundreds of researchers and dozens of labs and companies around the world.

Jasper Butcher, Rohith Krishna, Raktim Mitra, Seth M. Woodbury, Donghyo Kim, Rafael I. Brent, Yanjing Li, Nathaniel Corley, et al.
Science, 2026 · Co-first author
